Privacy Policy

Personal Assistant — Oura Integration

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Purpose

Personal Assistant — Oura Integration is a private, non-commercial application designed to retrieve personal health and wellness information from an authorized Oura account for use in automated daily briefings.

2. Information Accessed

With the account owner’s authorization, the application may access:

* Daily sleep summaries and sleep scores
* Readiness and recovery measurements
* Daily activity summaries
* Basic account information required for authentication

3. How Information Is Used

The information is used exclusively to generate personal morning, midday, and evening wellness summaries.

The application is not offered to other users and does not sell personal health information.

4. Storage and Processing

The intended implementation uses Google Apps Script to retrieve and process authorized Oura information.

Authentication credentials and tokens will be stored in restricted project configuration or user-specific storage. Health summaries may be stored temporarily to support automated briefings.

Information will not be intentionally published in the public GitHub repository.

5. Third-Party Services

The integration uses Oura and Google services. Apple Shortcuts may retrieve the resulting summaries.

Each service operates under its own applicable privacy terms.

6. Security

The application is intended to restrict access to the account owner. Reasonable safeguards will be implemented to protect credentials and personal health information.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner can revoke Oura authorization and discontinue the integration.

Stored credentials and retained health summaries can be deleted from the relevant services.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application’s functionality or data-handling practices change.
